Settings & data notes

Settings

Bot scans
Included by default so totals match the Uniqode dashboard. Exclude them to see human scans only.
Bots: included

Every number on this dashboard comes from Uniqode — no estimates, no stale snapshots.

Scan counts

  • Each physical scan is counted once (Uniqode's webhook sends duplicate events; they are deduplicated).
  • Bot scans (flagged by Uniqode as automated traffic) are included by default, so totals match the Uniqode dashboard exactly. Switch them off above to see human scans only.
  • Days are bucketed in Pacific time, matching the Uniqode dashboard.
  • Every scan is recorded from April 7, 2026, when the webhook went live. Before that there's only a partial backfill, so ranges reaching further back undercount. All-time totals are Uniqode's own and always exact.

Locations

  • Fitness Courts are placed from Salesforce (each Fitness Court's Site geo location). A few Salesforce doesn't have are placed by hand from NFC's planning maps.
  • Trail Line signs are placed from the Design Lab station layer, matched by the station ID in the code's name.
  • Codes with no known location aren't guessed onto the map; they're listed under Needs Location Verification in the rankings panel.

AI summaries

City and pilot overviews are written by Claude from the numbers shown on the page — never estimates. The label shows when each was written.

Trust check

On every load, each code's archive total is reconciled against Uniqode's official all-time count. The status line at the bottom of the map shows how many match.

Unmapped codes — verify location

These codes have no map position yet (their names couldn't be auto-placed). Add a lat/lon or an address to put each on the map. Saving writes the location into that code's Uniqode metadata.
